VERDICT accept-with-changes

1. **P0 — Integrity is being confused with acceptance.** Timestamps prove existence by a time, not invoice authenticity, capture completeness, or truth of local dates. Backdated invoices can be sealed today. Replace the verdict with “Inhalt stimmt mit dem spätestens am … belegten Stand überein”; distinguish locally sealed, externally dated, and qualified. For Finanzamt acceptance, require documented receipt-to-booking reconciliation, scan checks, original authenticity evidence, readable auditor export and restore drills. Commission an Austrian auditor’s review of the actual workflow; RKSV resemblance is no approval. [BMF](https://www.bmf.gv.at/themen/steuern/fuer-unternehmen/umsatzsteuer/informationen/elektronische-rechnung-ueberblick-ueber-die-aenderungen-durch-das-abgabenaenderungsgesetz-2012.html).

2. **P0 — Chains cannot prove completeness.** An owner can remove a suffix, restart the archive, or present competing valid branches. Add committed archive ID, sequence, predecessor and explicit genesis; reject duplicate sequences/forks. An independently retained expected head detects rollback only through that checkpoint. S3 receipts alone cannot establish the latest head; locked objects can be hidden by delete markers. Require live version enumeration and independently known witness identity, or report “Vollständigkeit extern nicht belegt”. [AWS](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonS3/latest/userguide/object-lock.html).

3. **P0 — TSA verification is not a 40-line security boundary.** Validate response status, CMS content type/signed attributes, actual signer/ESSCertID(v2), imprint algorithm/value, critical exclusive timestamping EKU, policy, chain and historical revocation. Generate a fresh request nonce per transaction, persist the request before sending, and match responses; the bag salt is separate. Replays must not become new evidence. `certReq` does not guarantee intermediates. Pin explicit trust policy, not an OS-root union; preserve authenticated historical Trusted Lists for qualification. Missing validation material means indeterminate. Test certificate injection and malformed attributes. [RFC3161](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3161.html), [DSS](https://ec.europa.eu/digital-building-blocks/DSS/webapp-demo/doc/dss-documentation.html).

4. **P0 — One Bitcoin header is insufficient.** Self-declared difficulty plus valid PoW does not establish main-chain membership or trustworthy time. Verify the OTS path against Bitcoin Core, or a validated header chain with explicit trusted checkpoint; explorer verification has additional trust. Label block time approximate. Persist pending proofs immediately; retry upgrades with backoff and retain completed `.ots` files. Multiple calendars share Bitcoin’s trust root. Audit and pin/fork [nbd-wtf](https://github.com/nbd-wtf/opentimestamps), rather than rewrite; fuzz bounded parsing and cross-test against the [reference client](https://github.com/opentimestamps/opentimestamps-client). Restrict calendar URLs and redirects to prevent SSRF.

5. **P0 — Tombstones do not authenticate predecessor fields.** A surviving seal hash cannot authenticate copied JSON without its preimage. Move chain fields into a minimal `chain.json` covered by the tagmanifest; retain both exact files after payload deletion. Record deletion as a subsequently anchored event. This preserves linkage, not deleted-content verification. Remove guaranteed unlinkability claims: backups, exported manifests and retained identifiers can preserve links despite deleting one nonce.

6. **P1 — The renewal formula strands evidence.** SHA-512 manifests behind a SHA-256 root do not provide independent SHA-512 protection. Yearly rehashing of tokens omits validation material and cannot repair an already broken algorithm. Specify interoperable [RFC4998](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc4998.html) renewal covering prior evidence and validation data, with new-algorithm rehashing of original data before weakness. Certificate expiry alone does not invalidate historical evidence. Keep renewal reminders and exportability in v1; do not advertise 22-year verification without a tested preservation path.

7. **P1 — Tighten the BagIt profile.** Keep ZIP as transport and the exact tagmanifest-byte hash as root. Tag manifests must exclude all tag manifests; avoid circularity. BagIt percent-escapes `%`, CR and LF, so generic `sha256sum -c` compatibility is false. Specify supported names and verification. Reject traversal, absolute paths, duplicate ZIP entries, Windows reserved names and reparse points; bound sizes. ZIP64 needs extra fields: replace “no extra fields” with an allowlist. [RFC8493](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8493.html).

8. **P1 — Persistence needs transactions.** Read-only attributes and “append-only JSON” do not constrain administrators. Stream hashes from the bytes actually archived; detect concurrent input changes. Use archive locking, temporary files, flush/atomic commit and crash recovery. Version the evidence schema, bind every record to the root, retain raw `.tsr`/`.ots` export, and atomically replace sidecars without discarding old evidence. Publish only commitments, never filenames or whole sidecars.

9. **P1 — Retention is underspecified.** Seal year is not necessarily document year; two classes are not universal. Capture relevant accounting periods, explicit reviewed retention dates and holds; mixed packages inherit the longest requirement. Include backup copies in deletion policy. Defer destructive deletion until authenticated chain remnants and restore behavior work.

10. **P2 — Prefer a pinned stable Wails v3 release for this greenfield shell.** [v3.0 is released](https://github.com/wailsapp/wails/releases/tag/v3.0.0), and requires Go 1.26+. Keep pure-Go guarantees scoped to core/CLI: v3’s CGO-free macOS/Linux backends remain [experimental](https://v3.wails.io/blog/wails-v3-1-released/). v2 fallback must include [2.17.1’s security fix](https://github.com/wailsapp/wails/releases). Fyne/Tauri add no compelling advantage here. Test installation, keyboard access and recovery; deny remote navigation and privileged rendering of invoice HTML.

11. **P2 — Hide implementation vocabulary and cut integrations.** Show one Archiv with dated “Ablagen”; Nachweise are automatic details. Offer “Ordner archivieren”, “Prüfen”, “Für Prüfung exportieren”; use `2026-11_000042_Rechnungen.zip` with matching evidence basename, identity inside. CLI: `archive`, `verify`, `status`, `export`; maintenance under `evidence update`. Start with one free TSA plus fallback, OTS and optional qualified TSA. Defer S3 adapters, custom renewal syntax and elaborate ZIP reproducibility; prioritize durable capture and a single actionable status.
