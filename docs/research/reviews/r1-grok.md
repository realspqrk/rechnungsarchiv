VERDICT changes: 8

A qualified timestamp and backups do not fulfil BAO §§131–132, UStG §11 and §18, UGB §212, the E-Rechnung-UStV or the GDPR. The token proves one time and one hash.

1. **§11 is a control procedure.** E-Rechnung-UStV §1 treats authenticity and integrity as satisfied by an internal control with a reliable audit trail, or by USP/PEPPOL, a qualified signature or seal, or EDI. UStR Rz 1564g requires that procedure to be documented. Rz 1570: issuer and recipient keep authenticity, integrity and legibility until retention ends. **Fix:** procedure chapters for receipt, match to supply and payment, byte-identical storage, and signature/EDI/PEPPOL evidence kept with the invoice. Art. 41 does not replace §11.

2. **Art. 41(2) is narrower than the UI.** The presumption covers accuracy of the date and time and integrity of the bound data, here the Prüfwert. Settings text “Gesetzliche Vermutung der Richtigkeit von Zeit und Inhalt” treats the invoice as correct. “Später jederzeit auf qualifiziert umstellbar” fails for old Ablagen: ADR-0001 keeps the old token, and a later stamp does not move the first time back. **Fix:** “Vermutung nur für Zeitpunkt und Unversehrtheit des Prüfwerts (Art. 41 Abs. 2). Ältere Ablagen behalten ihren Stempel.”

3. **Qualified status at genTime is specified and not built, so bankruptcy matters.** ADR-0004 judges status at genTime. Historical EU Trusted List checks are still open in `docs/dev/followup.md`. Art. 24(2)(h) and (i) require records after the QTSP stops, under a termination plan its supervisor checks. That is not a spare `.tsr`, and the period is not 7 or 22 Austrian years. A lost token is not reissued. A Spanish or Slovak QTSP is usable: Art. 41 applies in every Member State; supervision stays in the provider’s state. Disig BASE units state a time-stamp validity of at least one year; three years is the access package. Renewal is cut from v1. **Fix:** show “qualifiziert” only when the Nachweis stores the chain, genTime revocation data and Trusted List history. Docs: one QTSP suffices for recognition; the company keeps the token; validity must cover 7 or 22 years.

4. **Scans must stay unchangeable.** UStR Rz 1566 and §132 Abs 2 BAO: paper may go only if the copy stays complete, orderly, content-identical and true to the original, and cannot be changed afterwards. Examples: microfilm, optical disc, WORM. The read-only bit is convenience. “Ausstehend” means the binding time is still ahead, and the holder can delete the Nachweis and stamp again. The chain misses loss of the last Ablage. **Fix:** check the scan, stamp, then destroy the paper; do not replace an Ablage; investigate a second stamp. Do not call the ZIP “schreibgeschützt” as Unveränderbarkeit.

5. **Format, duplicates, cash receipts.** Store ZUGFeRD, XRechnung and ebInterface as received. UStR allows conversion only where the kept data show no content change. UStR Rz 1565 (2012 maintenance text): copies the issuer releases must be marked, or the VAT shown is owed because it was invoiced. §132a BAO (from 1 Oct 2026) only governs how a cash receipt reaches the payer. **Fix:** procedure lines on original format, marked duplicates, and “Kassenbelege und DEP nicht in diese Ablage”.

6. **The retention date is too crude.** §132 Abs 1 BAO: seven years from the end of the calendar year the voucher relates to; a non-calendar financial year shifts the start to the calendar year in which that year ends; pending proceedings extend the duty. §18 Abs 10 UStG: 22 years for the listed property records, 12 for certain WGG transfers. §18 Abs 12: 10 years for the platform records in Abs 11. UGB §212 Abs 1 also covers books, inventories, accounts and letters, which this tool does not store. One Belegjahr from the newest file date, default 7, and a display-only Sperre miss the rest. “10 Jahre (Verjährung)” cites §207 BAO, which is limitation, not retention. **Fix:** user sets the year of the record; warn on mixed years; add classes 12 and 10; call the extra 10 years optional; Sperre extends the date and blocks deletion.

7. **The audit needs findable, readable records.** §132 Abs 3 BAO: at the company’s cost and in reasonable time, provide the means to read the records and, where needed, a durable copy on a data carrier. §131 Abs 1 Z 5: vouchers kept so the entries can be checked. A BagIt ZIP and a Windows exe are not an invoice index for 2048. Backups do not show that the chain’s tail held every invoice. **Fix:** export supplier, number, date, amount and path; state that XML needs a viewer and that the books come from the accounting system; put an annual restore test in the procedure.

8. **GDPR.** Art. 5(1)(e) and Art. 17 require erasure when the duty ends, unless a proceeding or another basis remains (Art. 6(1)(c), Art. 17(3)(b)). Deletion is absent in v1. The Prüfwert carries no filename. “Nach außen geht nur der Prüfwert” still omits account name and IP, and default DigiCert is outside the EU. **Fix:** ROPA note: basis Art. 6(1)(c); EU QTSP as recipient; no US TSA without a Chapter V tool; delete only after Aufbewahrung-Bis, with a tombstone so other bags still verify.
